# Market Potential & Commercialization (B2B / B2G)

NetworkCheck functions as a civic utility for citizens and a high-value connectivity intelligence oracle for institutions:

### 1. Telecommunications Tower Operators & Infracos (IHS Towers, Helios)
- Identify micro-clusters experiencing dropped calls or antenna azimuth misalignments without sending costly field drive-test vehicles.

### 2. Commercial Banks, FinTechs & POS Agents (Moniepoint, OPay, Palmpay)
- POS terminal failure rates correlate heavily with sudden mobile network degradation. NetworkCheck data feeds enable smart agent terminal routing to alternative SIMs.

### 3. International NGOs, Field Research Teams & Health Outreach
- Immunization logistics, relief distribution, and election observers rely on knowing real-time communication stability across remote LGAs before deployment.

### 4. Regulatory Authorities (NCC & State Governments)
- Supplement periodic drive-test audits with continuous, ground-level citizen experience feedback.
